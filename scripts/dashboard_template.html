Source: People's Daily (人民日报) via Papersnake/people_daily_news; Chinese-language keyword matching (aliases and English translations in docs/entity_aliases.csv). An article counts once per entity however many times it is mentioned; entity groups overlap (e.g. "美国总统奥巴马" counts for the USA, President and Obama). Weekly periods start on Monday (the first and last weeks of the data are partial). Keyword counts measure attention, not tone; the sentiment views (when present) come from a language-model score of each article's excerpt around the mentions.
